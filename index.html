About

MedicAlgo is a freelance research and development practice providing algorithm design, data science, and artificial intelligence services to medical device and biotech companies.

With deep expertise in electrophysiology, signal processing, and biomedical data analysis, MedicAlgo bridges the gap between clinical science and engineering — turning complex physiological signals into actionable, regulatory-ready algorithms.

Whether you are developing a novel sensing device, building a real-time monitoring system, or seeking to integrate AI into your clinical workflow, MedicAlgo brings the scientific rigor and technical depth your project demands.

Services

Algorithm Development

Design and implementation of signal processing and detection algorithms for physiological data — from concept through verification and validation.

Data Analysis & Modeling

Statistical analysis, predictive modeling, and insight extraction from clinical and preclinical datasets. Rigorous methodology for regulatory submissions.

AI & Machine Learning

Deep learning, classification, and pattern recognition applied to biomedical signals. From neural network architecture to deployment-ready inference pipelines.

Electrophysiology

Specialized expertise in cardiac, neural, and muscular electrophysiology signal analysis — EEG, ECG, EMG, and beyond.
